# EKANNTO V3 — Local-first features

Includes comparison (2–4 products), followed shops and their products, product sharing from V2, notifications from V2, address book, guest cart from V2, FAQ/local ticket drafts, partial Bangla navigation translation, dark theme, estimated loyalty points, gift-card integration placeholder, seller verification disclosure, local product report drafts, and PWA manifest/service worker.

## Limitations
- No real identity verification or verified badges.
- Reports/tickets are **not transmitted** to support or moderation.
- Loyalty points are estimates only, no real balance or redemption. Gift card payment/redemption unavailable.
- Full Bangla translation incomplete. PWA installability varies by browser and may require PNG icons.
- All login/password data and user data are local demo only and must not be used with real credentials.
- No backend, AI, payment, email, push notification, or cross-device sync.
- A real secure backend is mandatory before public launch.

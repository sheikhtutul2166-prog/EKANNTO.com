# EKANNTO V44 — Feature Placement

Existing capabilities are linked from the business area where sellers would naturally look for them. Duplicate menu entries were avoided.

- Core Business: Products, Add Product, Orders, Inventory & Stock, POS, Followers & Following, Seller Wallet & Payouts.
- Orders & Delivery: Screenshot to Order + Bengali/English/Arabic OCR, Courier & Delivery, Courier Hub + one-tap dispatch + API/OAuth, Returns & Return Tracking, Invoices & Shipping Labels.
- Customers & Growth: Customer CRM, Customer Risk & Fraud Check + shared seller reports + advance-payment recommendation, AI Customer Replies, Promo Codes, Product Boost, Product Share Links, Instant Website Builder + platform storefront/no-hosting flow.
- Finance & Reports: Finance & Income Tracker, Expense Center, Profit & Loss, Analytics with daily/monthly charts, Reports.
- AI Studio: Seller AI, EKANNTO AI, AI Business Insights, AI Commerce OS, AI Marketing Studio with short/full/reels/story formats + emojis/hashtags, AI Photo Studio, Background Remover.
- Other Business Tools: Store Settings, Staff, Self Resell, Lottery.

Capabilities that are not separate destinations remain inside their matching module: unlimited order acceptance, offer-price badges, no-domain/hosting platform link, automated income/expense calculation, and return tracking.

The new Product Share Links page is a real dashboard option; it lists the seller's products and provides a copyable product URL for each one.

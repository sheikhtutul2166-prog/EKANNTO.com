EKANNTO V53.3 — Receipt & Seller Wallet fixes

- Withdrawal receipt logo sizing/fit improved for mobile and PDF.
- QR verification area enlarged; QR library retry improved and fake QR pattern is not presented as a valid code.
- PDF page-break rules updated to reduce split receipt sections.
- Completed withdrawals are included when calculating seller available balance.
- Admin approval synchronizes seller walletBalance with the approved withdrawal deduction.

Note: QR scan verification requires the receipt URL to be hosted and reachable online, and the QRCode library to load. This browser-only build does not provide server-side verification.
